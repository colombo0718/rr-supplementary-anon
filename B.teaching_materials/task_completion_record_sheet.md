# Task Completion Record Sheet

**Purpose:** Classroom observation. The TA records each student's task completion status and time, one student at a time.  
**Instructions:** When a student finishes a task, they raise a hand; the TA checks the screen and enters the completion time in the cell (e.g., 14:47).  
Enter "✗" for not completed, and "M" (make-up) if completed after class.
